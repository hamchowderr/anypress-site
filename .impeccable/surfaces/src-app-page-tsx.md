# Homepage (anypress.io /)

Scope: marketing homepage, Persuade mode. Audience: every customer group equally, mostly non-technical, with the technical side (sellers, resellers, developers) reached from the sell band and the system diagram. Action: pick a site type and start (checkout on shop.anypress.io). Proof: none on hand; never invent any. Constraints: plain words, no tech terms on the homepage.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Sketchbook (owner-approved round 3, 2026-10-24). A friendly sketchbook on a white desk: every idea gets a hand-drawn object that draws itself, with handwritten margin notes, and the logo's ribbon colours only in the hatching and hard offset shadows. See DESIGN.md.

STORY (top to bottom): announcement bar, hero (headline with one highlighted word, price, three ticks, "built for" line, floating drawn scene, four-step flow), "included" marquee, the add-on maze comparison table, what are you building (five types), example sites (tabs with real screenshots), your part / our part, the system behind it (one storefront, every order its own website), who it's for, sell band, promises, price summary, questions, final call to action, big footer.

FIRST VIEWPORT: centered headline "Your business deserves a real website.", start and prices buttons, ticks, and the floating drawn scene.

FORM: sketchbook, hand-drawn rough.js objects (scripts/drawings.mjs).
