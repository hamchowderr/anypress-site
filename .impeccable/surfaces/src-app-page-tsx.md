# Homepage (anypress.io /)

Scope: marketing homepage, Persuade mode. Audience: every customer group equally, mostly non-technical. Action: pick a site type and start (checkout on shop.anypress.io). Proof: none on hand; never invent any. Constraints: plain words, no eyebrows, no gradient text, no tech terms on the homepage.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is a flat-pack assembly booklet with a twist: the hard steps are ours. You pick, add your own things and open for business; we put it together, keep it running and grow it with you. It refuses the category-default SaaS hero, screenshot, logo row and three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Each step is a full-bleed page in one ribbon colour, running in order amber, orange, red, rose, magenta, then ink. Black line-art diagrams in one stroke weight. Big step numerals in an expanded grotesque, a parts list with quantities, and "You" / "Us" tabs on each step. Buttons are thick-outlined labels, like the stickers on a box.

STORY: Building a site is a box of loose parts. Here, you pick a box and we put it together, open it for you and keep it running. Every dollar is yours and the site is yours. Visitors pick a site type and start.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On the left, a giant headline: "Pick a website. We'll put it together." Under it, step 1 is the working control: five boxes (site type, what it's for, price a month), each linking to start. On the right, an exploded line drawing of a website (desktop only; phones go straight to the five boxes). The same parts slide together as step 2 scrolls into view. Ground is amber.

FORM: assembly manual, candidate 7 of 7, seed key 76ffbd6e (degraded roll, no challengers).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
