## Platform

web

## Users

Every customer group is spoken to equally; none is the "main" audience (owner, 2026-10-19). The groups: online shops and niche site sellers, supplement/CBD and other regulated sellers, course creators and educators, chambers and associations, franchises and multi-location brands, agencies and freelancers, SaaS and software teams.

What they share: they need a real website to run their business, most are not technical, and they don't want to become their own web team. Each group gets its own dedicated page; the homepage does not use a picker to swap content.

## Product Purpose

AnyPress is WordPress, managed for you. A customer picks what kind of site they need (website, bookings, online store, community and courses, or everything), and AnyPress builds it on its own WordPress with the right tools already set up, then keeps it running, backed up and secure.

Origin (owner's 2024 wildcloud interview, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnlAOk0Eg-8): WordPress is powerful but feels like "a giant flea market" of plugins, plus hosting hurdles. AnyPress exists to take that away so businesses get the power of WordPress without the mess.

Success: a non-technical visitor understands what they get in one scroll and starts a site.

## Positioning

Building a website shouldn't be hard. Other platforms make it harder than it needs to be, or lock you into their tools and take a cut of your sales. AnyPress gives you real WordPress, set up and looked after for you, with no cut of your sales, and the site stays yours.

- Customer sites live at <name>.anypress.site until the customer connects their own domain.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five site types with owner-chosen starting prices per month: Website $12, Bookings $19, Online store $29, Community & courses $39, Everything $79. Every type comes in Small, Medium (2x) and Large (4x); yearly is 10x monthly; 3+ sites save 10/20/30% at 3, 10 and 25 sites.
- Every site: its own WordPress and database, SSL, nightly backups with restore, free .anypress.site address, connect your own domain (one click with supported DNS providers), email from your own domain, one-click sign-in to WordPress, no AnyPress fee on sales (Stripe's card fee applies).
- Tools by type come from the Fluent suite (FluentCart, FluentCRM, FluentBooking, FluentCommunity, Fluent Forms, FluentAffiliate, Fluent Support) plus Rank Math. Name them only on deeper pages.
- Anyone can sell websites under their own name: as a seller (one shop, pays per site) or a reseller (several shops, one wholesale bill). Agencies are one of the groups who do this; developers can create sites through an API (deeper pages only).
- Not offered / not to claim: page caching or speed claims, automatic core updates, 24/7 support, uptime guarantees, done-for-you migration (moving a WordPress site in is self-serve with the AnyPress Export plugin), AI content tools, templates library.

## Product Principles

1. Easy beats powerful-but-confusing: say what the visitor can do, not how it works.
2. The site is yours: real WordPress, your domain, your sales, no lock-in.
3. Done for you: we set it up and look after it.
4. Every kind of business is welcome; no group comes first.
