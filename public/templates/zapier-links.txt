Zapier share-a-copy links: not available

The two AnyPress demo Zaps can't be shared as copies, for two reasons
(Zapier help, "Share a copy of your Zap", checked 2026-10-24):

1. Shared Zap templates are deprecated. "You can no longer create new shared
   Zap templates." Existing shared-template links stop working on
   December 15, 2026. Zapier points to guided templates instead:
   https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/43465487495181
2. "Zap workflows with ... private apps ... cannot be shared as copies."
   AnyPress (Zapier app 248504) is a private, invite-only integration.

The Share option is no longer in the Zap editor menu or the Zaps list menu.

The two Zaps, for reference:
- Create site: Stripe "New Payment" -> AnyPress "Create Site" (Zap 384643434)
- Site ready: AnyPress "Site Event" -> Email by Zapier "Send Outbound Email" (Zap 384643509)

People can install AnyPress with the invite link and build these two Zaps
themselves:
https://zapier.com/developer/public-invite/248504/d92ccd4f604ea3e76535ff8452c69eb9/
